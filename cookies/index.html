How this site uses browser storage

The core website does not use advertising or audience-measurement cookies. It uses an optional third-party chat widget supplied by Chatbase, which may use cookies or similar browser storage to operate the conversation experience.

External services

If you use the chat widget, submit an enquiry or follow an external link, the relevant provider may process technical information under its own terms and privacy practices. You can avoid using the chat widget and contact Roibéard directly by email instead.

Managing cookies

You can review or remove cookies and stored site data through your browser settings. Blocking third-party storage may affect the chat widget but should not prevent you from reading the website or using the email contact link.

Questions

For questions about this website's use of cookies or similar technologies, email contact@roibeardcoughlan.com.
