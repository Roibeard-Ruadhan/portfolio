Where value leaks out

The problem is rarely a lack of software.

It is usually the gaps between people, documents and systems: the same information re-entered, work waiting in inboxes, or decisions made without the full picture.

→

Admin absorbs good people

→

Knowledge gets scattered

→

Handoffs create risk

From opportunity to working solution

Identify the opportunity. Design the right solution. Build it properly.

Not every workflow needs automation or a custom platform. The right solution depends on the work, the value at stake and the complexity the team can sensibly support.

01 / Process improvements

Remove a recurring time drain

Redesign a contained part of the work so it takes less attention and is easier to carry out consistently.

  • Email and admin workflows
  • Meeting actions and follow-ups
  • Document sorting and extraction
  • Clearer templates and ownership

02 / Connected systems

Close the gaps between tools

Design and build reliable handoffs that move information through existing systems with clear checks and ownership.

  • CRM and invoice workflows
  • Follow-up and status automation
  • Approvals and notifications
  • System integrations

03 / Custom solutions

Build around a distinctive process

Design and build purpose-made tools where an important workflow cannot be served sensibly off the shelf.

  • Searchable project knowledge
  • Document comparison and review
  • Structured operational assistants
  • Human-reviewed decision support

Where an opportunity is worth pursuing, I design, build and implement the solution — whether that means improving the process, connecting existing systems, building an automation or applying AI where it adds real value.

These are examples of opportunities, not claims about completed client projects. Any solution depends on your process, data and controls.

About Roibéard
